# Flexite System Assessment - Stakeholder Map

**System:** Flexite (Incident Management)  
**Assessment Date:**  2026-01-08  

---

## Primary Stakeholders

### Jan-Eric - Health & Safety Manager / Super User (also Sofia in his team)

**Role:** Flexite super user and primary system administrator  
**Tenure:** 3 years at Kubal (since 2023); 8 years with Flexite (since 2017)  
**Usage Frequency:** Daily  
**System Criticality Rating:** 9/10

**Responsibilities:**
- Incident investigation and analysis
- Government compliance reporting (Arbetsmiljöverket)
- Safety policy management and enforcement
- Preventive action planning and tracking
- Management reporting and dashboards
- System administration and user support
- Contact point for government agencies

**Pain Points:**
- Manual government reporting (12 reports/month to e-tjänster)
- Manual dashboard creation (30-45 minutes per cycle)
- Manual LTA report generation
- Multi-team coordination for preventive actions

**System Satisfaction:** High (recommends investing more)  
**Influence Level:** High (safety compliance authority)

**Key Quote:**
"It's critical to control the company to be aligned on safety compliance by Swedish law. System helps us keep information which helps create preventive procedures which really reduce incidents."

---

### All Employees - Incident Reporters

**Role:** Primary system users for incident reporting  
**Population:** All Kubal employees  
**Usage Frequency:** As needed (generates 120-140 monthly reports)  
**Access Method:** Mobile app and web form

**Responsibilities:**
- Report accidents, near-misses, unsafe conditions, property damage
- Provide incident details and evidence
- Complete assigned corrective actions

**Pain Points:**
- Some fear consequences from reporting (being addressed through education)
- Multi-team coordination requirements for complex incidents

**System Satisfaction:** High (100% reporting compliance achieved)  
**Influence Level:** Medium (collective user adoption drives system value)

**User Behavior:**
- Prefer using Flexite over calling/emailing
- Even when calling, still required to use system
- Strong adoption indicates trust in system

---

### Department Managers - Responsible Persons

**Role:** Incident reviewers and corrective action owners  
**Usage Frequency:** Weekly to daily (varies by department)

**Responsibilities:**
- Review incidents in their departments
- Assign and track corrective actions
- Respond to incident notifications
- Complete assigned preventive action plans
- Close out incidents after resolution

**Pain Points:**
- Multi-team coordination for preventive actions
- Time-intensive incident analysis requirements

**System Satisfaction:** Good (usually respond promptly to assignments)  
**Influence Level:** Medium-High (incident resolution depends on their engagement)

---

### Senior Management - Safety Oversight

**Role:** Safety performance monitoring and strategic decision-making  
**Usage Frequency:** Monthly or as-needed

**Responsibilities:**
- Review safety performance dashboards
- Monitor compliance status
- Make strategic safety investments
- Respond to serious incidents
- Support safety culture initiatives

**Pain Points:**
- Manual dashboard creation delays insights
- Limited real-time visibility into safety trends

**System Value:**
- Compliance assurance
- Incident trend visibility
- Preventive action effectiveness
- Regulatory audit readiness

**Influence Level:** High (budget and investment decisions)

## Stakeholder Communication Strategy

**For System Changes:**

**Must Engage:**
- Jan-Eric: System owner, must approve all changes
- All Employees: Communication on new features or process changes
- Department Managers: Training on new workflow capabilities
- Senior Management: ROI justification and strategic alignment

**Should Inform:**
- IT Department: Technical requirements for integrations
- Maintenance Team: If Idus integration implemented
- HR/Payroll: If Agda/Visma integration implemented

**May Coordinate:**
- Arbetsmiljöverket: If government integration affects reporting process
- Insurance Providers: If claims integration implemented
