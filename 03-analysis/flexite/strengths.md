# Flexite System Assessment - Strengths

**System:** Flexite (Incident Management)  
**Assessment Date:** 2026-01-08  
**Super User:** Jan-Eric Berglund   
**System Criticality Rating:** 9/10

---

## Core System Strengths

### 1. Excellent User Adoption and Compliance

**Evidence:**
- 100% incident reporting rate through system
- Employees prefer using Flexite over calling/emailing (by control rule of Säkerhet/skydd avdelning)
- All employees have access to report incidents
- Even when employees call, they're required to use Flexite
- Strong adoption since implementation

**Business Value:**
Complete incident visibility ensures comprehensive safety management and regulatory compliance. High user adoption means the system is fulfilling its core purpose effectively.

---

### 2. High-Volume Processing Capability

**Evidence:**
- Handles 120-140 monthly incident reports consistently
- Processed 1,572 reports in 2025
- Manages approximately 12 government agency reports per month
- Daily usage by safety administrator without performance issues

**Business Value:**
System scales effectively with Kubal's safety reporting needs and maintains performance under consistent workload.

---

### 3. Excellent System Performance and Reliability

**Evidence:**
- Performance rating: Excellent (speed and responsiveness)
- Minimal IT support requirements (only rare system access issues)
- High system availability
- No mobile access issues
- No reported downtime or performance complaints

**Business Value:**
Reliable system ensures continuous safety reporting capability and minimizes IT support costs.

---

### 4. User-Friendly Interface

**Evidence:**
- Ease rating: Easy for employees to report incidents
- Jan-Eric describes it as "easy to use and not complex"
- Positive feedback from regular users
- Low training requirements
- Accessible via mobile app and web form

**Business Value:**
Low barrier to entry ensures employees report incidents promptly without technical obstacles.

---

### 5. Strong Audit Trail and Compliance Capabilities

**Evidence:**
- Maintains adequate audit trails for investigations
- Easy compliance demonstration to auditors and inspectors
- Fully meets Arbetsmiljöverket (Swedish Work Environment Authority) requirements
- Supports 24-hour government reporting requirements
- Provides complete documentation for investigations

**Business Value:**
System ensures regulatory compliance and provides defensible documentation for legal and audit purposes. Critical for avoiding penalties and demonstrating due diligence.

---

### 6. Effective Preventive Action Tracking

**Evidence:**
- Good tracking of preventive actions using system data
- Data enables creation of preventive procedures that reduce incidents
- Easy tracking of corrective action completion
- Automated notifications to responsible persons work reliably
- Can view pending tasks and send reminders to managers

**Business Value:**
System moves beyond reactive incident logging to enable proactive safety improvements that reduce future incidents.

---

### 7. Automated Workflow Features

**Evidence:**
- Automatic notifications to responsible persons when assigned actions
- Email notifications work reliably
- Responsible persons usually respond promptly to assigned incidents
- Workflow supports incident lifecycle from report to closure

**Business Value:**
Automation reduces manual follow-up burden and ensures timely incident resolution.

---

## Strategic Strengths

### Critical for Safety Compliance
Jan-Eric rates system criticality at 9/10, stating: "It's critical to control the company to be aligned on safety compliance by Swedish law. System helps us keep information which helps create preventive procedures which really reduce incidents."

### Business Continuity Support
While production could continue without Flexite, safety compliance control and alignment would be difficult even with manual management. System has backup capabilities.

### Foundation for Safety Culture
- High reporting rate indicates employees trust the system
- Easy-to-use interface removes reporting barriers
- Data-driven approach enables continuous safety improvements
- Jan-Eric actively addressing reporting culture concerns through education

---

## Competitive Advantages

**Compared to Manual Processes:**
- Automated notifications vs. manual follow-up
- Centralized data vs. scattered records
- Audit trail vs. paper documentation
- Trend analysis capability vs. retrospective reporting

**System Maturity:**
- Jan-Eric has 8 years experience with Flexite (since 2017)
- 3 years at Kubal demonstrates system longevity
- Proven track record handling high incident volumes

---

## Preservation Priorities

**Must Preserve During Any System Changes:**
1. User-friendly interface and high adoption rate
2. Mobile and web accessibility
3. Excellent system performance and reliability
4. Strong audit trail and compliance capabilities
5. Automated notification workflows
6. Preventive action tracking effectiveness

**Investment Strategy:**
Build on these strengths by adding automated reporting and system integrations while maintaining the core functionality that drives 100% user compliance.

---

**Key Insight:**
Flexite's core incident management functionality is excellent. The system's weaknesses are not in what it does, but in what it doesn't connect to (integration gaps) and what it makes users do manually (reporting/analytics).
