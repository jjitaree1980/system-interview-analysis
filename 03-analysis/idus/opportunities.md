**Impact Potential:** Medium (if keeping Idus)

- Reduce training costs (users already familiar)
- Lower change management risk
- Build on user familiarity
- Evolutionary approach vs. revolutionary change
- Super user already advocates for this path

#### CRITICAL CAVEATS AND CONSIDERATIONS

**BIAS ALERT:** This opportunity heavily influenced by super user's preference for known system. Critical considerations:

**1. Claimed "Core Strengths" Are Standard CMMS Features:**
- Graphical interface: Available in ALL modern CMMS (Planon, Ultimo, Fiix, eMaint)
- BOM hierarchy: Basic functionality in ANY professional CMMS
- Comprehensive asset database: Core purpose of CMMS, not unique strength
- Verdict: No genuine competitive advantages identified

**2. Change Management Cost Must Compare to Opportunity Cost:**
- Yes, change is costly and risky
- BUT continued waste (4.7M SEK/year) and technical debt also costly
- Question: Which is more expensive over 5 years?

**3. "User Familiarity" Includes Users Who Bypass System:**
- Super user familiar and advocates keeping Idus
- But blue-collar workers bypass system (not familiar/comfortable)
- Production team sometimes bypasses system
- "Familiarity" may apply mainly to super user, not all users

**4. Modern CMMS May Be EASIER to Adopt:**
- Cloud-based systems like Fiix, eMaint designed for intuitive use
- Modern UX design may require LESS training than Idus
- Mobile-first design natural for field workers
- Assumption that "change = harder" may be wrong

**5. Sunk Cost Fallacy Risk:**
- Past investment in Idus NOT relevant to future 5-year decision
- Question: What's best path forward, not what's been invested historically
- Don't let past investments drive future poor decisions

**6. Internal Upgrade Capability Questioned:**
- Claimed "easy to upgrade internally"
- But version upgrade significantly delayed
- SAP integration broken for extended period
- May overestimate internal capability

#### Requirements to Capture
**Only Pursue If Opportunity 1 Recommends Keeping Idus:**

- Maintain graphical interface in all upgrades
- Preserve BOM hierarchy structure
- Protect comprehensive asset database
- Enhance capabilities (mobile, reporting, analytics)
- Communicate continuity to users

**If Opportunity 1 Recommends Replacement:**
- Ensure replacement has equivalent or better capabilities
- Plan careful data migration
- Design change management program
- Leverage lessons learned from Idus

#### Balanced Decision Framework

**Keep Idus IF:**
- Opportunity 1 evaluation shows no materially better alternatives exist
- Total Idus investment (upgrades + modules + waste) < replacement TCO over 5 years
- Change management risk assessed as extremely high
- Idus vendor roadmap aligns with future needs and shows product investment

**Replace Idus IF:**
- Modern alternatives offer materially better capabilities (especially if included standard)
- 5-year TCO of replacement < 5-year TCO of Idus + modules + waste
