# Stakeholder Map - Agda PS

## Overview

**System:** Agda PS (Payroll System)
**Mapping Date:** December 2025
**Primary Source:** Interview with Helena (Super User)

---

## Primary Stakeholders

### 1. Helena - Super User & Payroll Manager

**Role:** Primary system administrator and payroll processor

**System Usage:**
- Daily access, approximately 8 hours per day
- 7 years total Agda PS experience (2.5 years with Kubal organization)
- Manages all transactions from entry through payroll completion

**Responsibilities:**
- Process monthly payroll (1.5 week cycle)
- Review and validate time data
- Generate reports for Ekonomi team
- Coordinate with Visma support when needed
- Train and support other users

**Pain Points:**
- Manual review of new shift time calculations (October 2025 change)
- Manual data generation for banking and accounting systems
- Some employees still submit paper leave requests
- Occasional incomplete time data requiring follow-up

**Strengths/Satisfaction:**
- System saves significant time
- Payroll process is easy and accurate
- Excellent vendor support
- Strong compliance handling
- System stability and reliability

**Influence Level:** Very High - Primary decision-maker and system advocate

**Sentiment:** Highly Positive - Strongly recommends continued investment

**Key Quote:**
> "Definitely invest more" [when asked about continuing with Agda PS]

---

### 2. New HR Team Member (Recently Recruited)

**Role:** Secondary payroll administrator (backup/coverage)

**System Usage:**
- Will work with Agda PS alongside Helena
- Has several years of Agda PS experience from previous role
- Will provide two-person coverage for business continuity

**Responsibilities:**
- Share payroll processing duties with Helena
- Provide coverage during absences
- Support system operations

**Current Status:**
- Recently recruited
- Onboarding to organization structure (already knows system)

**Needs:**
- Learn Kubal's specific organizational structure and workflows
- Understand company-specific configurations

**Influence Level:** Medium - Will become co-decision-maker

**Sentiment:** Unknown (not yet interviewed)

---

### 3. HR Team

**Role:** Core system users

**System Usage:**
- Daily/regular access to core HR functions
- Primary users of employee/personal information management

**Responsibilities:**
- Manage employee data and personal information
- Process manual leave requests from some employees
- Input and maintain HR records

**What They Value:**
- Having all employee/personal information in one centralized place
- System consolidation and data accessibility

**Pain Points:**
- Manual input for employees who submit paper leave requests
- Some data entry duplication

**Influence Level:** Medium - Regular users with operational input

**Sentiment:** Positive - Appreciate centralized information

**Key Quote:**
> "They like to get all employee/personal info in one place"

---

### 4. Management Team

**Role:** Self-service approval users

**System Usage:**
- Regular access for approval workflows
- Self-service portal for time attendance and leave approvals

**Responsibilities:**
- Review and approve time attendance
- Review and approve leave requests
- Monitor team time data

**System Interaction:**
- Use approval functions in self-service module
- Do not interact with core payroll or HR functions

**Influence Level:** Medium - Stakeholders in approval processes

**Sentiment:** Neutral/Positive - No complaints reported

---

### 5. Employees

**Role:** Minimal self-service users

**System Usage:**
- Periodic access for time logging
- Monthly access to view salary slips
- Occasional leave planning (vacation, parental leave)

**Responsibilities:**
- Log work time and attendance
- Submit leave requests
- Review salary information

**Pain Points:**
- Some employees not comfortable with computers
- Some prefer paper processes due to old habits

**Usage Patterns:**
- Time logging is the most-used module
- Some still submit leave requests on paper instead of using self-service
- Occasional incomplete time data entries

**Influence Level:** Low - End users with limited input

**Sentiment:** Mostly Positive - Few complaints; issues mainly related to computer literacy

**Key Quote:**
> "As they are minimal usage group, they do quite ok and did not much complain about the system. Just few but it was because they are not use to the computer"

---

### 6. Ekonomi Team

**Role:** Data recipients (downstream system users)

**System Usage:**
- Do not directly use Agda PS
- Receive manual data exports from Helena

**Responsibilities:**
- Upload payroll data to banking system
- Process accounting data from payroll
- Handle financial aspects of payroll

**Current Process:**
- Receive manually generated data reports from Helena
- Upload data to bank system manually
- Process payroll data in accounting system

**Pain Points (Potential):**
- Manual data receipt and upload
- Potential for data transfer errors
- Coordination dependency on HR timing

**Influence Level:** Medium - Affected by integration gaps

**Sentiment:** Unknown (not interviewed)

**Note:** This stakeholder group should be interviewed to understand their perspective on manual data transfers

## Stakeholder Needs & Priorities

### Critical Needs (Must Have)

**All Stakeholders:**
- System reliability and uptime during payroll periods
- On-time salary payments (rated 10/10 criticality)
- Compliance with Swedish regulations

**Helena & HR Team:**
- Accurate payroll calculations
- Easy data entry and processing
- Centralized employee information
- Minimal manual workarounds

**Employees:**
- Access to salary information
- Simple time logging
- Easy leave request process

**Management:**
- Efficient approval workflows
- Visibility into team attendance

### Important Needs (Should Have)

**Helena:**
- Automated shift time calculation validation
- Banking system integration
- Accounting system integration
- Reduced manual data transfers

**HR Team:**
- Improved employee adoption of self-service
- Onboarding module functionality

**Ekonomi Team:**
- Direct system integration for data transfer
- Reduced manual upload processes

### Nice to Have (Could Have)

**All Users:**
- Enhanced reporting capabilities
- Additional training and documentation

## Key Relationships & Dependencies

### Critical Dependencies

**Helena → Employees:**
- Depends on employees logging time accurately and completely
- Paper leave requests create manual work for Helena
- Incomplete data blocks Helena's payroll processing

**Helena → Ekonomi Team:**
- Manual data transfer creates coordination dependency
- Timing of data delivery affects Ekonomi team workflow

**Helena → Visma Support:**
- Depends on rapid issue resolution for business continuity
- Currently very strong relationship with excellent support

**All Stakeholders → Helena:**
- Single point of dependency for payroll processing
- Being addressed through addition of second HR team member

### Integration Points

**Agda PS → Time Tracking System:**
- Successfully integrated (working well)

**Agda PS → Banking System:**
- Not integrated (manual process via Ekonomi team)

**Agda PS → Accounting System:**
- Not integrated (manual process via Ekonomi team)

**Agda PS → Government Agencies:**
- Automated compliance reporting (working well)

## Recommendations for Stakeholder Management

### Immediate Actions

1. **Interview Ekonomi Team**
   - Understand their perspective on manual data transfers
   - Assess impact of integration gaps on their workflow
   - Gather requirements for banking/accounting integration

2. **Engage New HR Team Member**
   - Conduct interview once onboarded
   - Document their system experience and suggestions
   - Capture fresh perspective on processes

3. **Address Helena's Dependencies**
   - Accelerate two-person coverage implementation
   - Document critical workflows for business continuity
   - Reduce single-point-of-failure risk

### Medium-Term Actions

4. **Employee Adoption Campaign**
   - Survey employees on self-service experience
   - Address computer literacy barriers
   - Reduce paper leave requests through change management

5. **Integration Planning**
   - Work with Ekonomi team and Visma on banking integration
   - Prioritize highest-impact automation opportunities
   - Develop implementation roadmap

6. **Vendor Relationship**
   - Maintain strong Visma partnership
   - Discuss shift calculation validation
   - Explore onboarding module capabilities
