# Threats - Agda PS

## Overview

**System:** Agda PS (Payroll System)
**Analysis Date:** December 2025
**Source:** Interview with Helena (Super User)

This document identifies potential threats, risks, and challenges that could impact system effectiveness, business operations, or user satisfaction.

---

## 1. Single Point of Dependency

**Category:** Business Continuity Risk
**Severity:** High
**Likelihood:** Medium (Being mitigated)
**Potential Impact:** Critical - Payroll processing could be disrupted

### Threat Description
Helena is the primary system expert with deep knowledge of Agda PS and payroll processes. Loss of Helena (resignation, illness, extended leave) could severely impact payroll operations, which is rated 10/10 in business criticality.

### Current Risk Level
Medium and decreasing - New HR team member with Agda PS experience has been recruited to provide two-person coverage.

### Potential Consequences
- Inability to process payroll on time
- Employees not receiving salaries
- Regulatory compliance issues
- Loss of institutional knowledge
- Rushed training of replacement staff
- Potential legal and financial penalties

### Warning Signs
- Helena expressing job dissatisfaction
- Increased workload or stress
- Lack of documentation for critical processes
- Delayed establishment of two-person coverage

### Mitigation Strategies (Current)
- Recruiting second HR team member with Agda PS experience (in progress)
- Cross-training to establish backup coverage

### Additional Mitigation Recommendations
- Accelerate knowledge transfer to new team member
- Document all critical payroll workflows
- Create step-by-step procedure guides
- Establish regular coverage rotation
- Maintain relationship with Visma for emergency support
- Consider succession planning

### Residual Risk After Mitigation
Low - Once two-person coverage is fully operational

## 2. Manual Process Errors

**Category:** Operational Risk
**Severity:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Potential Impact:** Moderate - Data errors, processing delays, rework

### Threat Description
Manual data transfers between systems (banking, accounting, pension reporting) create opportunities for human error. Errors in payroll data could result in incorrect payments, compliance issues, or financial discrepancies.

### Current Risk Level
Medium - Managed through Helena's experience and careful review, but risk persists

### Potential Consequences
- Incorrect salary payments to employees
- Banking transfer errors
- Accounting misstatements
- Pension reporting errors
- Regulatory compliance violations
- Employee dissatisfaction
- Time-consuming error correction
- Potential legal penalties

### Warning Signs
- Increasing frequency of data entry corrections
- Employee complaints about payment errors
- Reconciliation discrepancies
- Ekonomi team reporting data issues

### Mitigation Strategies (Current)
- Helena's careful review and validation
- Established workflows and checklists
- Regular reconciliation

### Additional Mitigation Recommendations
- Prioritize banking system integration (highest risk area)
- Implement automated data validation rules
- Add secondary review process for high-value transactions
- Document standard operating procedures
- Create error tracking and root cause analysis process
- Provide additional training to Ekonomi team on data handling

### Residual Risk After Mitigation
Low - Integration would eliminate most manual transfer risks

## 4. Resistance to Digital Adoption

**Category:** User Adoption Risk
**Severity:** Low
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Potential Impact:** Low to Medium - Reduced system value, manual workarounds

### Threat Description
Some employees continue using paper-based processes despite self-service capabilities. This resistance could persist or spread, reducing system value and requiring ongoing manual workarounds.

### Current Risk Level
Low - Limited impact, but persistent pattern

### Potential Consequences
- Continued manual data entry for HR
- Underutilization of purchased system features
- Reduced ROI on system investment
- Incomplete or delayed data
- Employee dissatisfaction if forced to change
- Inconsistent processes across workforce

### Warning Signs
- Increasing number of paper leave requests
- Low self-service portal usage rates
- Employee complaints about digital requirements
- Younger employees also avoiding digital tools
- Growing backlog of manual data entry

### Mitigation Strategies (Current)
- Accepting current state with minimal impact
- HR team handles manual entry as needed

### Additional Mitigation Recommendations
- Conduct employee survey to understand barriers
- Provide computer literacy training
- Create simple, visual user guides
- Implement gradual change management approach
- Highlight benefits of self-service (faster approvals, convenience)
- Consider generational differences in approach
- Assign digital champions to encourage peers
- Make self-service mandatory for new hires

### Residual Risk After Mitigation
Low - Limited business impact even if adoption remains incomplete

## 5. Regulatory Compliance Changes

**Category:** Compliance Risk
**Severity:** High (if not managed)
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Potential Impact:** High - Legal penalties, operational disruption

### Threat Description
Swedish payroll regulations (Skatteverket, Försäkringskassan, pension rules, collective agreements) change periodically. Failure to implement changes correctly could result in compliance violations and penalties.

### Current Risk Level
Low - Well-managed through automated Visma updates

### Potential Consequences
- Regulatory violations and penalties
- Incorrect tax withholding
- Social insurance calculation errors
- Pension miscalculations
- Legal action against company
- Reputational damage
- Retroactive corrections required
- Employee trust issues

### Warning Signs
- Delayed Visma updates
- New regulations Visma doesn't support
- Complex customizations interfering with updates
- Audit findings or regulatory notices
- Calculation discrepancies

### Mitigation Strategies (Current)
- Visma automatically updates system for regulatory changes
- Strong compliance track record
- Expert vendor support for custom requirements

### Additional Mitigation Recommendations
- Stay informed about upcoming regulatory changes
- Test updates in non-production environment when possible
- Maintain relationship with compliance experts
- Document all customizations that might affect compliance
- Regular compliance audits or reviews
- Subscribe to regulatory update services
- Maintain clear communication channel with Visma for urgent changes

### Residual Risk After Mitigation
Very Low - Strong current management of this risk

## 7. Data Security and Privacy Risks

**Category:** Security Risk
**Severity:** High (if breached)
**Likelihood:** Low
**Potential Impact:** Critical - Legal, financial, reputational damage

### Threat Description
Payroll data contains highly sensitive personal and financial information. Data breaches, unauthorized access, or privacy violations could have severe consequences under GDPR and Swedish data protection laws.

### Current Risk Level
Low - No issues reported, but inherent risk with sensitive data

### Potential Consequences
- GDPR violations and fines (up to 4% of annual revenue)
- Employee privacy breaches
- Identity theft affecting employees
- Reputational damage
- Loss of employee trust
- Legal action from affected individuals
- Regulatory investigations
- Mandatory breach notifications

### Warning Signs
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Unusual data export activities
- Weak access controls
- Lack of audit trails
- Sharing of login credentials
- Non-compliance with data retention policies

### Mitigation Strategies (Current)
- Visma-managed security infrastructure
- Limited user access (Helena and new team member)
- Established vendor security practices

### Additional Mitigation Recommendations
- Regular security training for all Agda PS users
- Strong password policies and multi-factor authentication
- Regular access reviews and deprovisioning
- Audit log monitoring
- Data classification and handling procedures
- Incident response plan for potential breaches
- Regular security assessments
- Compliance with Visma's security recommendations
- Limit data export capabilities
- Encrypt sensitive data transmissions
- Regular backup and recovery testing

### Residual Risk After Mitigation
Low - With comprehensive security practices

## 8. Cost Escalation

**Category:** Financial Risk
**Severity:** Low to Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Potential Impact:** Medium - Budget pressure, reduced investment capacity

### Threat Description
Visma licensing, support costs, or costs for additional modules (onboarding, integrations) could increase beyond budget expectations. This could pressure budgets or force reduction in system capabilities.

### Current Risk Level
Low to Medium - No current issues, but general vendor pricing risk

### Potential Consequences
- Budget overruns
- Reduced investment in other HR initiatives
- Inability to add desired features (onboarding module)
- Delayed integration projects
- Management pressure to find alternatives
- Forced reduction in user licenses or features

### Warning Signs
- Significant price increases at renewal
- Additional fees for standard features
- Premium pricing for integrations
- Reduced volume discounts
- Changes in licensing model
- Unexpected implementation costs

### Mitigation Strategies (Current)
- Only purchasing needed modules (no unused features)
- Strong system value justifies current investment

### Additional Mitigation Recommendations
- Understand pricing structure and renewal terms
- Negotiate multi-year agreements for price stability
- Monitor total cost of ownership (licensing + support + customization)
- Calculate ROI for each feature and module
- Maintain market awareness of alternative pricing
- Build business case for new features before committing
- Budget for expected price increases
- Consider module bundling for cost efficiency

### Residual Risk After Mitigation
Low - Good cost management and clear value delivery
