# Agda PS - Annual Waste Cost Analysis

## Calculation Methodology

**Assumptions:**
- 12 payroll cycles per year (monthly)
- Average HR/payroll hourly rate: 400 SEK/hour
- 52 working weeks per year

---

## Quantified Waste Costs

### 1. Manual Banking Data Transfer
**Time:** 1-2 hours per payroll cycle × 12 cycles = 12-24 hours/year
**Cost:** 4,800-9,600 SEK/year

### 2. Manual Accounting Data Transfer  
**Time:** 1-2 hours per payroll cycle × 12 cycles = 12-24 hours/year
**Cost:** 4,800-9,600 SEK/year

### 3. Shift Calculation Manual Review (Temporary)
**Time:** 1-2 hours per payroll cycle × 12 cycles = 12-24 hours/year
**Cost:** 4,800-9,600 SEK/year

### 4. Paper Leave Requests & Manual Entry
**Time:** 2-4 hours per week × 52 weeks = 104-208 hours/year
**Cost:** 41,600-83,200 SEK/year
*(This is the largest waste cost)*

### 5. Manual Pension Data Generation
**Time:** ~0.5 hours per cycle × 12 cycles = 6 hours/year
**Cost:** 2,400 SEK/year

### 6. Incomplete Time Data Follow-up
**Time:** ~0.5 hours per cycle × 12 cycles = 6 hours/year
**Cost:** 2,400 SEK/year

---

## Total Annual Waste Cost

**Low Estimate:** ~61,000 SEK/year
**High Estimate:** ~117,000 SEK/year

**Mid-Range:** ~**90,000 SEK/year**

---

## Waste Cost Breakdown by Priority

### High-Impact Waste (Addressable Soon)
- Paper leave requests: 41,600-83,200 SEK/year (46-71%)
- Banking integration: 4,800-9,600 SEK/year (8-8%)
- Shift calculation: 4,800-9,600 SEK/year (8-8%)

**Subtotal:** 51,200-102,400 SEK/year (84-87% of total waste)

### Medium-Impact Waste
- Accounting integration: 4,800-9,600 SEK/year (8%)

### Low-Impact Waste
- Pension data: 2,400 SEK/year (2%)
- Incomplete data: 2,400 SEK/year (2%)

---

## Key Insights

1. **Self-service adoption is the largest waste** - Nearly half of waste comes from employees not using available features

2. **Integration gaps create consistent waste** - Banking and accounting transfers happen every cycle

3. **Most waste is addressable** - 84-87% of waste can be eliminated through known solutions

4. **Current waste is relatively low** - For a mission-critical system handling ~100+ employees, 90,000 SEK/year is manageable

---

## ROI of Addressing Waste

If all waste areas are addressed:
- **Potential savings:** 61,000-117,000 SEK/year
- **Implementation costs to consider:**
  - Banking integration: One-time setup cost
  - Self-service training: Minimal cost
  - Shift validation: Included in Visma support

**Payback period:** Likely under 1 year for most improvements
