## 1. General Overview & Current Usage

### Q: How long have you been the super user for Agda PS, and what is your primary role beyond managing the system?

### Q: What HR and payroll functions does Agda PS handle?

**A:** Travel expense, payroll, time tracking, self-service, recruitment, statistik for salary survey

**Notes:**

### Q: How many employees' data does this system manage?

**A:** Employee cound is around 300-500.


## 2. Technical & Functional Aspects

### Q: What would happen if Agda PS was unavailable during payroll week?

**A:** It would be critical, as employees would not receive their salary on time. Since Helena has been working with the system, there has never been that kind of problem.

**Notes:**
- Payroll processing takes approximately 1.5 weeks to prepare and complete
- The Agda support team typically takes around 1 business day to resolve technical issues


### Q: How critical is this system? Why?

**A:** Rating: **10/10** – It is essential to pay salaries on time.


### Q: How does the system handle compliance and statutory reporting?

**A:** The system fully handles data compliance with Swedish regulations, including:
- Skatteverket (Swedish Tax Agency)
- Social fees
- Pensions
- Försäkringskassan (Swedish Social Insurance Agency)
- Collective agreement fees (kollektivavtalade avgifter)


### Q: How frequently do you access the system? And who are the primary users?

**A:** Helena accesses the system daily, approximately 8 hours per day.

**Primary user groups:**
- **HR team** – Uses core functions in the system
- **Management** – Uses self-service for approval of time and attendance
- **Employees** – Minimal usage group; uses the system for time logging and checking salary slips

**Additional notes:**
- Time logging is the most frequently used module
- Kubal has purchased only the functions they need, so there are no unused modules


## 3. User Experience & Workflows

### Q: How easy is it to process payroll? Any regular pain points?

**A:** The payroll process is very easy and the data is accurate.

There are no regular pain points, but there is one area that requires additional attention and manual review before processing payroll. The company recently changed shift working times, which started in October 2025. Currently, Helena needs to manually review whether the time calculations for this new shift are correct.


### Q: What do HR team members appreciate most about Agda PS?

**A:** They appreciate having all employee/personal information in one place.


### Q: How intuitive is it for new HR staff?

**A:** The system complexity is moderate, but as the new person has good knowledge of the system, she only needs to learn the organization's structure.


### Q: How satisfied are employees with self-service features?

**A:** As they are minimal users, they are doing quite well and have not complained much about the system. There have been a few complaints, but these were due to users not being comfortable with computers.

**Notes:**
- The system works well, and the processes/workflows save Helena a lot of time
