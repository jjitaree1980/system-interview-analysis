# Speaker Notes

## Presentation Guide

---

### Before the Presentation

- [ ] Test screen sharing / projector
- [ ] Have backup of presentation
- [ ] Prepare printed handouts of executive summary
- [ ] Review Q&A preparation notes
- [ ] Confirm attendee list

---

### Slide-by-Slide Notes

#### Slide 1: Title
*Keep this brief - just introduce yourself and the topic*

---

#### Slide 2: Agenda
- Set expectations for the meeting
- Mention Q&A at the end (or throughout, depending on preference)
- Note that detailed report is available for those wanting more depth

---

#### Slide 3: Project Overview
- Emphasize the "why" - business drivers for this analysis
- Briefly explain the four systems if not all attendees are familiar
- Mention interview count to establish credibility

---

#### Slide 4: Interview Summary
- Highlight the breadth of perspectives gathered
- Mention any key roles that provided particularly valuable insights
- Note that interview notes are available as appendix

---

#### Slide 5-7: Key Findings
- Lead with impact, not technical details
- Use specific quotes where they add impact
- Connect findings to business outcomes
- Pause for questions if this is a critical finding

---

#### Slide 8-11: System Summaries
- Keep each system to 2-3 minutes maximum
- Focus on what matters to this audience
- Be prepared to go deeper if asked
- Use the rating to set expectations

---

#### Slide 12: Comparison Matrix
- Don't read every cell - highlight key differences
- Point out unexpected findings
- Connect back to business impact

---

#### Slide 13: Integration Landscape
- Explain the visual before diving into details
- Highlight critical integration gaps
- Note any quick win integration opportunities

---

#### Slide 14: Risk Summary
- Be direct about risks - don't minimize
- Explain the mitigation approach
- Offer to discuss specific risks in detail

---

#### Slide 15-18: Recommendations
- Prioritize based on audience interest
- Be prepared to discuss alternatives
- Have cost/effort estimates ready
- Connect each recommendation to findings

---

#### Slide 19: Implementation Roadmap
- Emphasize this is a proposal, not a commitment
- Highlight dependencies
- Note what decisions are needed to proceed

---

#### Slide 20: Investment Summary
- Be prepared for detailed cost questions
- Have comparable industry benchmarks if available
- Discuss ROI expectations

---

#### Slide 21: Next Steps
- Be specific and actionable
- Assign owners if possible
- Set follow-up timeline

---

#### Slide 22: Q&A
- Encourage questions
- If you don't know an answer, commit to follow up
- Watch for time

---

### Common Questions to Prepare For

1. **"What's the total cost of the recommendations?"**
   - Have a range ready
   - Break down by priority level

2. **"Can we do this with existing staff?"**
   - Be realistic about resource needs
   - Suggest alternatives if resources are constrained

3. **"What's the risk of doing nothing?"**
   - Have specific risks ready to cite
   - Quantify if possible

4. **"How confident are you in these findings?"**
   - Reference interview count and diversity
   - Note any areas of uncertainty

5. **"What do users actually want?"**
   - Have key quotes ready
   - Reference specific pain points

6. **"Why didn't we include [other system]?"**
   - Explain scope decisions
   - Offer to include in future analysis
