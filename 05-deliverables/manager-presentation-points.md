## 3. PAIN POINTS & GAPS - "What Hurts and Why"

### Pain Point 1: Idus (Maintenance) - Single Point of Failure & Data Gap

**The problem:**
- All maintenance work orders funnel through ONE person (Tom)
- Shift leaders can't enter data directly into Idus
- They use Excel spreadsheets instead, then hand paper reports to Tom
- SAP integration reportedly broken - causing manual rework

**Why it matters:**
- If Tom is unavailable, maintenance work orders stop
- Double data entry wastes time
- Equipment issues can take 2-3 days to reach the system
- Estimated ~687K SEK/year in Excel shadow workflow waste alone

**The catch:**
- These findings come from Tom only (the super user)
- We have LOW confidence - no other users were interviewed
- We don't know if the real problem is the system or the access permissions

---

### Pain Point 2: Excel Shadow Workflows - Hidden Cost

**The problem:**
- Shift leaders maintain personal Excel sheets BEFORE entering data into formal systems
- Same data gets entered twice (Excel first, then system)
- Some observations may never leave Excel

**The revealing contrast:**

| | Idus (maintenance) | Flexite (safety) |
|--|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Same shift leaders** | Use Excel workarounds | Enter data directly |
| **System access** | Don't have it | Have it |
| **Adoption** | Poor | 100% |

> **Key insight for your manager:** The same people who "can't use systems" are successfully using Flexite every day. The problem is likely ACCESS, not capability. This could mean a much cheaper fix than a full system upgrade.

---

### Pain Point 3: Agda/Visma - Feature Underutilization

**The problem:**
- Employees don't use self-service features (shift preferences, digital payslips, schedule swaps)
- Helena (HR) handles requests manually that the system could automate
- ~75K SEK/year in unnecessary manual work

**The good news:** This is a training problem, not a system problem. Easy fix.

---

### Pain Point 4: Flexite - Limited Analytics & Manual Reporting

**The problem:**
- System records data but doesn't analyze it
- Jan-Eric does all trend analysis manually
- Government reports require manual export and reformatting

**Context:** This is a low-priority issue. Jan-Eric's manual analysis is actually excellent, and the system meets its compliance purpose. This is "nice to improve" not "need to fix."

## 5. OPPORTUNITIES TO IMPROVE - "What Can We Do"

### PRIORITY 1 - DO NOW (Low Risk, Validated)

**Agda/Visma Training Campaign**
- **Investment:** 25K SEK
- **Expected return:** 75K SEK/year savings
- **Payback period:** ~4 months
- **Risk:** Low - validated with the super user
- **What:** Group training sessions on self-service features
- **Timeline:** Q1 2026

> This is the easiest win. Clear ROI, low cost, immediate benefit.

---

### PRIORITY 2 - INVESTIGATE NOW (High Impact, Needs Data)

**Idus Validation Study**
- **Investment:** ~50-95K SEK for study
- **Potential savings:** 1.69M - 2.90M SEK/year (IF waste figures are confirmed)
- **Risk of NOT doing the study:** Investing blindly in the wrong solutions
- **What the study covers:**
  - Interview 8-10 actual Idus users (not just Tom)
  - Test SAP integration with IT team
  - Track shift leader Excel usage for 2 months
  - Compare with modern CMMS alternatives
  - Pilot: Give 2-3 shift leaders direct Idus access
- **Timeline:** Month 1-3, then decide at Month 4

> **The key argument:** We're being asked to make a significant investment based on one person's perspective. Spending 50-95K to validate before committing makes the final decision much stronger.

---

## 7. TALKING POINTS FOR COMMON QUESTIONS

**"Why can't we just fix Idus now?"**
> We only have one person's perspective. The waste estimates range from 1.69M to 2.90M - that spread tells us we don't really know the scope. A 3-month study ensures we invest in the right things.

**"Is this a crisis?"**
> No. 3 of 4 systems perform well. Ascendo is best-in-class. This is about optimization, not firefighting.

**"What about the Excel problem?"**
> Shift leaders use Excel because they lack Idus access - but the SAME people use Flexite directly with 100% compliance. The fix might be as simple as granting system access + training, which is much cheaper than a full upgrade.

**"What does success look like?"**
> Ascendo is our internal proof. Perfect SAP integration, 95-100% OCR accuracy, zero manual reconciliation. It shows our IT team CAN achieve excellent integration. We use it as the benchmark.

**"What's the total cost?"**
> Immediate: 25K (Agda training) + 50-95K (Idus study) = 75-120K SEK
> Compare to: Investing without validation could mean spending significantly more on the wrong priorities

**"What's the timeline?"**
> Month 1-3: Run Idus validation study + launch Agda training
> Month 4: Present findings, make Idus investment decision
> Month 5+: Execute chosen path with confidence
