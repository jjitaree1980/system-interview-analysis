# Question Rationale - Flexite

## Overview

This document explains the reasoning behind each interview question for Flexite, helping interviewers understand the purpose and guide deeper exploration.

---

## Section 1: General Overview

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| What is your role, and how does it involve Flexite? | Establishes context for interpreting responses. Different roles have different perspectives and needs. |
| In your own words, what is Flexite used for? | Reveals user's mental model of the system. Misalignment indicates training gaps. |
| How often do you use the system? | Usage frequency affects expertise level and feedback weight. |
| How did you learn to use the system? | Identifies training effectiveness and knowledge transfer practices. |
| Who do you contact for questions? | Maps the informal support network and identifies knowledge holders. |

---

## Section 2: Current Usage & Workflows

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Walk me through a typical workflow | Reveals actual vs intended usage. Identifies inefficiencies. |
| What are the 3 most important features? | Identifies core functionality that must be preserved. |
| Are there features you don't use? | Reveals underutilized capabilities or poor fit. |
| How does Flexite integrate with other tools? | Maps the system ecosystem and integration pain points. |
| Workarounds developed? | Workarounds indicate unmet needs or poor UX. |

---

## Section 3: Pain Points & Challenges

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Biggest frustrations with Flexite? | Direct identification of problems. |
| If you could change one thing? | Forces prioritization of improvements. |
| Significant issues or outages? | Identifies reliability concerns. |
| Tasks that take longer than they should? | Identifies efficiency opportunities. |
| Manual work that should be automated? | Identifies automation opportunities. |

---

## Section 4: Data & Reporting

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Confidence in data accuracy? | Data trust is fundamental to system value. |
| Reports generated - do they meet needs? | Identifies reporting gaps. |
| Getting data out for other purposes? | Reveals integration needs. |
| Data the system doesn't capture? | Identifies capability gaps. |

---

## Section 5: Support & Training

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Where do you go for help? | Maps support ecosystem. |
| Adequately trained? | Training gaps reduce system value. |

---

## Section 6: Future State

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| What capabilities do you wish it had? | Forward-looking needs assessment. |
| Essential to keep in replacement? | Identifies non-negotiable features. |
| Anything else to share? | Open-ended catch-all. |

---

## Interview Tips

1. **Follow the energy** - Explore topics that generate strong reactions
2. **Ask "why" repeatedly** - Get to root causes
3. **Look for patterns** - Note alignment with other interviews
4. **Capture quotes** - Verbatim statements are powerful
5. **Observe non-verbal cues** - They provide additional data
