# Question Rationale - Idus

## Overview

This document explains the reasoning behind each interview question for Idus, helping interviewers understand the purpose and guide deeper exploration.

---

## Section 1: General Overview

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| What is your role, and how does it involve Idus? | Establishes context for interpreting responses. Different roles have different perspectives and needs. |
| In your own words, what is Idus used for? | Reveals user's mental model of the system. Misalignment with actual purpose indicates training or communication gaps. |
| How often do you use the system? | Usage frequency affects expertise level and the weight we give to their feedback. Daily users have different insights than occasional users. |
| How did you learn to use the system? | Identifies training effectiveness and knowledge transfer practices. |
| Who do you contact for questions? | Maps the informal support network and identifies knowledge holders. |

---

## Section 2: Current Usage & Workflows

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Walk me through a typical workflow | Reveals actual vs intended usage. Identifies inefficiencies and workarounds. Most valuable for understanding real needs. |
| What are the 3 most important features? | Identifies core functionality that must be preserved in any changes. Helps prioritize future development. |
| Are there features you don't use? | Reveals underutilized capabilities that might benefit from better training or indicate poor fit. |
| How does Idus integrate with other tools? | Maps the system ecosystem. Integration points are often pain points. |
| Workarounds developed? | Workarounds indicate unmet needs or poor UX. These are opportunities for improvement. |

---

## Section 3: Pain Points & Challenges

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Biggest frustrations with Idus? | Direct identification of problems. Open-ended to avoid leading responses. |
| If you could change one thing? | Forces prioritization. The single most important improvement from user perspective. |
| Significant issues or outages? | Identifies reliability concerns and support quality. Major incidents often shape perceptions. |
| Tasks that take longer than they should? | Identifies efficiency opportunities. May reveal UX issues or missing features. |
| Manual work that should be automated? | Identifies automation opportunities. Often indicates gaps in system capability. |

---

## Section 4: Data & Reporting

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Confidence in data accuracy? | Data trust is fundamental. Low confidence undermines system value. |
| Reports generated - do they meet needs? | Reporting is often a pain point. Identifies gaps in BI capabilities. |
| Getting data out for other purposes? | Reveals integration needs and data accessibility issues. |
| Data the system doesn't capture? | Identifies capability gaps. May reveal workarounds using other systems. |

---

## Section 5: Support & Training

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Where do you go for help? | Maps support ecosystem. Reveals reliance on vendor vs internal resources. |
| Adequately trained? | Training gaps reduce system value and user satisfaction. |

---

## Section 6: Future State

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| What capabilities do you wish it had? | Forward-looking needs. Helps assess system's evolution potential. |
| Essential to keep in replacement? | Identifies non-negotiable features. Critical for any transition planning. |
| Anything else to share? | Open-ended catch-all. Often surfaces insights not covered by structured questions. |

---

## Interview Tips

1. **Follow the energy** - When an interviewee shows strong emotion about a topic, explore deeper
2. **Ask "why" multiple times** - Get to root causes, not just symptoms
3. **Look for patterns** - Note if issues align with other interviews
4. **Capture quotes** - Verbatim statements are powerful in analysis
5. **Observe non-verbal cues** - Frustration, hesitation, enthusiasm all provide data
