# Question Rationale - Agda/Visma

## Overview

This document explains the reasoning behind each interview question for Agda/Visma, helping interviewers understand the purpose and guide deeper exploration.

---

## Section 1: General Overview

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| What is your role, and how does it involve Agda/Visma? | Establishes context for interpreting responses. Different roles have different perspectives and needs. |
| In your own words, what is Agda/Visma used for? | Reveals user's mental model of the system. Misalignment with actual purpose indicates training or communication gaps. |
| How often do you use the system? | Usage frequency affects expertise level and the weight we give to their feedback. |
| How did you learn to use the system? | Identifies training effectiveness and knowledge transfer practices. |
| Who do you contact for questions? | Maps the informal support network and identifies knowledge holders. |

---

## Section 2: Current Usage & Workflows

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Walk me through a typical workflow | Reveals actual vs intended usage. Identifies inefficiencies and workarounds. |
| What are the 3 most important features? | Identifies core functionality that must be preserved in any changes. |
| Are there features you don't use? | Reveals underutilized capabilities or poor fit. |
| How does Agda/Visma integrate with other tools? | Maps the system ecosystem. Integration points are often pain points. |
| Workarounds developed? | Workarounds indicate unmet needs or poor UX. |

---

## Section 3: Pain Points & Challenges

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Biggest frustrations with Agda/Visma? | Direct identification of problems. Open-ended to avoid leading responses. |
| If you could change one thing? | Forces prioritization of the most important improvement. |
| Significant issues or outages? | Identifies reliability concerns and support quality. |
| Tasks that take longer than they should? | Identifies efficiency opportunities. |
| Manual work that should be automated? | Identifies automation opportunities. |

---

## Section 4: Data & Reporting

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Confidence in data accuracy? | Data trust is fundamental to system value. |
| Reports generated - do they meet needs? | Reporting is often a pain point in ERP/financial systems. |
| Getting data out for other purposes? | Reveals integration needs and data accessibility issues. |
| Data the system doesn't capture? | Identifies capability gaps. |

---

## Section 5: Support & Training

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Where do you go for help? | Maps support ecosystem. Visma's support quality is key for ERP systems. |
| Adequately trained? | Training gaps reduce system value and user satisfaction. |

---

## Section 6: Future State

| Question | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| What capabilities do you wish it had? | Forward-looking needs assessment. |
| Essential to keep in replacement? | Identifies non-negotiable features for transition planning. |
| Anything else to share? | Open-ended catch-all for additional insights. |

## Agda/Visma-Specific Considerations

As an ERP/financial system, pay particular attention to:
- Financial reporting accuracy and timeliness
- Compliance and audit requirements
- Integration with banking and payment systems
- Period-end processing and closing procedures
- Multi-currency and multi-entity handling (if applicable)
- Historical data access and retention
