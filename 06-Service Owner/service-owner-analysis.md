## Key Recommendations

### 1. PRIORITY: Transfer Flexite User Management to IT
**Impact:** Frees 14.4 hrs/month average from Safety team (up to 20 hrs during peaks)
**Effort:** 16.9 hrs/month average IT time (up to 27 hrs during peaks)
**Benefit:** Safety team focuses 100% on safety compliance instead of password resets
**Critical:** During peak onboarding (summer temps), Safety expertise needed for training, not for creating user accounts

### 2. Standardize User Management Across Systems
**Current problem:** Each business team handles user management differently
**Recommendation:** 
- IT owns all user account technical operations
- Business teams approve access levels based on role requirements
- Implement self-service password reset where possible
- Create streamlined onboarding process with templates for seasonal peaks

### 3. Clarify Technical vs Business Vendor Coordination
**Current problem:** Mix of technical and business vendor tickets
**Recommendation:**
- IT handles: System errors, integration issues, performance problems, infrastructure
- Business handles: Feature requests, workflow questions, business process discussions
- Create clear escalation criteria

### 4. Implement Seasonal Capacity Planning
**Problem:** Flexite user management has predictable seasonal peaks
**Solution:**
- Safety team notifies IT 2 weeks before bulk onboarding
- IT prepares batch processes for efficiency
- Document peak periods: sommarvikarier prep (~April-May annually)
- Template-based permission sets for common roles
- Reduces peak from 20 hrs to ~12-15 hrs through process efficiency

### 5. Leverage Ascendo Success Model
**Evidence:** Cloud-based + perfect integration = minimal IT burden
**Application:** When evaluating Idus replacement, prioritize:
- Cloud/SaaS delivery
- Vendor-managed infrastructure
- Proven integration reliability

## Risk Analysis

### Risks of IT Taking Service Owner Role

**Low Risk (Easy to transfer):**
- ✅ User account creation/removal - Pure technical
- ✅ Password resets - Technical process
- ✅ Technical troubleshooting - IT already does this

**Medium Risk (Requires coordination):**
- ⚠️ Permission management - Business must define access levels
- ⚠️ Technical vendor tickets - Need clear routing criteria
- ⚠️ First-line support - Need clear handoff from users to IT

**High Risk (Could create bottlenecks):**
- ❌ Report creation - Requires deep business context
- ❌ User training - Requires process knowledge
- ❌ Business configuration - Requires domain expertise

### Mitigation Strategies

1. **Create clear RACI matrix** for each task category
2. **Implement ticketing system** to route requests correctly
3. **Document permission approval workflows** before IT takes over execution
4. **Start with one system pilot** (recommend Flexite due to high impact)
5. **Monthly review meetings** for first 6 months to adjust processes
6. **Prepare for seasonal peaks** with advance planning and batch processes
