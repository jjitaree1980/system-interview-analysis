## Risk Analysis

### Risks of IT Taking Service Owner Role

**Low Risk (Easy to transfer):**
- ✅ User account creation/removal - Pure technical, well-defined
- ✅ Password resets - Technical process
- ✅ Technical troubleshooting - IT already does this
- ✅ Infrastructure management - IT already owns

**Medium Risk (Requires coordination):**
- ⚠️ Permission management - Business must define access levels based on role/compliance
- ⚠️ Technical vendor tickets - Need clear routing criteria to avoid business tickets going to IT
- ⚠️ First-line support - Need clear handoff from users to IT vs business teams

**High Risk (Could create bottlenecks):**
- ❌ Report creation - Requires deep business context
- ❌ User training - Requires process knowledge (HR policies, safety procedures, maintenance workflows)
- ❌ Business configuration - Requires domain expertise (financial controls, safety compliance, labor law)

### Mitigation Strategies

1. **Start small, prove value** - Flexite pilot demonstrates feasibility
2. **Clear communication** - Users need to know who to contact for what
3. **Document workflows** - Especially permission approval processes
4. **Regular reviews** - Monthly for first 6 months to adjust
5. **Success metrics** - Track business team time freed, IT response times, user satisfaction
