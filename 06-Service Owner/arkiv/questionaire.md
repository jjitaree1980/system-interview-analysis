# System Operations Assessment - Understanding Current State

**Purpose:** To understand how our systems currently operate and identify opportunities for improvement and better collaboration between teams.

**Systems to assess:** Ascendo, Idus, Flexite, Agda/Visma

---

## Current Operations Understanding

### Daily/Weekly Activities
- What are the regular tasks you perform to keep the system running smoothly?
- Which of these tasks are manual vs automated?
- Are there any repetitive tasks that take up significant time?
- What do you wish could be automated or simplified?

### Issue Management
- When problems occur, what's your typical process for resolving them?
- How do you track and document issues?
- Are there recurring problems that keep coming back?
- What types of issues are easiest vs most difficult to resolve?

### User Support
- What are the most common questions or requests you get from users?
- Which questions are about how to use the system vs technical problems?
- How do users typically contact you when they need help?
- Are there frequently asked questions that could benefit from better documentation?

---

## System Health & Monitoring

### Current Monitoring
- How do you know if the system is running well?
- Do you have alerts set up for problems, or do you discover issues reactively?
- How often do you proactively check system health?
- What early warning signs tell you something might go wrong soon?

### Backup & Recovery
- How are backups currently managed?
- Have you ever needed to restore data? How did that go?
- What's your confidence level in the current backup process?
- Are there any concerns about data safety or recovery?

---

## Changes & Updates

### Change Process
- How do you currently plan and implement changes?
- Do you have a way to test changes before they go live?
- How do you communicate changes to users?
- Have there been changes that caused unexpected problems? What happened?

### System Updates
- How often does the vendor release updates?
- What's your typical process for applying updates?
- How much time does update planning and implementation usually take?
- Are there updates you've postponed? Why?

---

## Integration & Dependencies

### System Connections
- Which other systems does this one connect to?
- How critical are these integrations to daily operations?
- Have integration issues caused problems? How often?
- How do you find out when an integration breaks?

### Cross-Team Coordination
- When you need help with this system, who do you typically contact?
- Are there tasks where you need to wait for other teams?
- What information do you wish you had better access to?
- Where do handoffs between teams sometimes get stuck?

---

## Peak Times & Critical Periods

### Business Criticality
- When is this system most critical for business operations?
- Are there times when downtime would be especially problematic?
- What business processes would stop if this system went down?
- How quickly does the system need to be back up if there's an outage?

### Workload Patterns
- Are there predictable busy periods (month-end, payroll, production peaks)?
- How does your workload vary throughout the month/year?
- Are there times when you need to be more available or responsive?
- Do you ever get called outside normal work hours? How often?

---

## Knowledge & Documentation

### Current Documentation
- What documentation exists for this system?
- Is the documentation up-to-date and useful?
- What's not documented that probably should be?
- If you were on vacation, could someone else handle basic tasks?

### Learning Curve
- How long did it take you to become comfortable managing this system?
- What was most difficult to learn?
- What business knowledge is needed to manage this system effectively?
- What would a new person need to know to support this system?

---

## Improvement Opportunities

### Pain Points
- What's most frustrating or time-consuming about managing this system?
- What breaks most often or causes the most disruption?
- If you could change three things about how this system is managed, what would they be?
- What keeps you up at night about this system?

### Support Needs
- Where do you feel you need more support or expertise?
- Are there skills or knowledge gaps that make your work harder?
- What would make your job easier when managing this system?
- Are there tools or resources you wish you had access to?

---

## Collaboration & Communication

### Current Team Structure
- Who else is involved in managing this system?
- How do you share responsibility? (backup coverage, on-call, etc.)
- How do you coordinate when multiple people are involved?
- Are there times when unclear ownership causes delays?

### IT Collaboration
- When do you currently work with IT on this system?
- What works well in the current collaboration?
- Where could collaboration be improved?
- What would you want IT to understand better about this system?

---

## Future Considerations

### Upcoming Changes
- Are there any planned upgrades, migrations, or major changes coming?
- What concerns do you have about future changes?
- What improvements or new features are users asking for?

### Capacity & Growth
- Is the system keeping up with business growth and demands?
- Are you seeing signs that capacity might become an issue?
- What would happen if user numbers or data volume doubled?

---

## Time Investment Assessment

### Current Time Spent
- In a typical week, roughly how much time do you spend on:
  - Routine maintenance and monitoring: _____ hours
  - User support and questions: _____ hours
  - Problem-solving and troubleshooting: _____ hours
  - Planning and implementing changes: _____ hours
  - Meetings and coordination: _____ hours
  - Documentation and reporting: _____ hours

### Time Variability
- What's a quiet week like? _____ total hours
- What's a busy week like? _____ total hours
- What's a crisis week like? _____ total hours
- How often are there crisis weeks? _____ per year

### Workload Distribution
- How much of the work could be done by someone with only technical knowledge?
- How much requires understanding of business processes?
- How much requires both?

---

## Open Discussion

- What questions should we have asked that we didn't?
- What else should we know about managing this system?
- What are your hopes for how this system could be better supported in the future?
- What concerns do you have about changes to how the system is managed?

---

## Next Steps

After completing this assessment:
1. Identify common patterns across systems
2. Find opportunities for better tools, automation, or processes
3. Determine where clearer collaboration between IT and business teams would help
4. Develop action plans for improvements that benefit everyone
5. Ensure any changes enhance support without disrupting what works well
