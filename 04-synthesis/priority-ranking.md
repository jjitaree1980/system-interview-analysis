## Prioritization Framework

### Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Business Impact | 30% | Effect on operations, revenue, efficiency |
| User Impact | 25% | Number of users affected, severity of pain |
| Strategic Alignment | 20% | Alignment with company goals |
| Feasibility | 15% | Technical and resource feasibility |
| Risk Mitigation | 10% | Reduces business or technical risk |

### Priority Levels

- **P1 - Critical:** Must address immediately
- **P2 - High:** Address within next quarter
- **P3 - Medium:** Plan for next 6-12 months
- **P4 - Low:** Consider for future
