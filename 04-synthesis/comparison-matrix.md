## Detailed Analysis

### What Works Well

**Idus Strengths**
- Reliable work order management and tracking for 1,800 monthly tickets
- Well-established preventive maintenance scheduling framework
- Mobile access enabling field-based maintenance work
- Proven stability with consistent performance across 30 users

**Agda/Visma Strengths**
- Robust and reliable payroll processing with strong accuracy record
- Comprehensive HR functionality covering full employee lifecycle
- Strong regulatory compliance meeting Swedish Arbetsmiljöverket requirements
- High data integrity and system stability
- Excellent vendor support responsiveness

**Comparison:** Both systems excel at their core functions - Idus for maintenance workflow management and Agda/Visma for HR/payroll processing. Agda/Visma demonstrates superior vendor support quality. Both systems have proven stability and reliability, earning user trust in mission-critical operations.

---

### Critical Pain Points

**Idus: Technical Failures Across Multiple Areas**
- Broken SAP inventory integration: 1,552,000 SEK annually (manual verification, workarounds)
- Missing reporting module: 176,000 SEK annually (2+ days monthly manual Excel work)
- No mobile platform: 981,000 SEK annually (field travel time, productivity loss)
- System performance issues: 627,000 SEK annually (slow response times)
- Incomplete work logging: 490,150 SEK annually (untracked quick fixes)
- No procurement integration: 387,000 SEK annually
- Additional waste categories: 517,440 SEK annually
- **Total estimated annual inefficiency cost: 4,730,590 SEK (~4.7M SEK)**

**Agda/Visma: Organizational Gaps and Manual Workarounds**
- Paper leave requests and manual entry: 41,600-83,200 SEK annually (largest waste - unused self-service)
- Shift calculation manual review: 4,800-9,600 SEK annually (temporary issue)
- Manual pension data generation: 2,400 SEK annually
- Incomplete time data follow-up: 2,400 SEK annually
- **Total quantified waste: 51,200-97,600 SEK annually (mid-range ~75,000 SEK)**

**Note:** Manual banking and accounting data transfers are intentional per Ekonomi team requirements for full verification control, not system limitations.

**Comparison:** Idus suffers from technical failures (broken integrations, missing modules) costing 4,730,590 SEK annually - significantly higher than Agda/Visma's 51,200-97,600 SEK waste. Idus's top waste driver is the broken SAP integration alone (1,552,000 SEK/year). Agda/Visma's primary issue is organizational (unused self-service features, lack of training) with paper leave requests representing 80-85% of total waste. Idus needs urgent technical fixes; Agda/Visma needs change management and training investment.

### Integration Status

**Idus: Critical Integration Failure**
- Broken SAP connection creating critical operational gap
- Limited API utilization for automated data exchange
- Manual Excel-based workarounds dominating inter-system communication
- Priority level: Critical - SAP restoration essential for operational efficiency

**Agda/Visma: Functional with Intentional Manual Controls**
- Banking and accounting data transfers are intentionally manual per Ekonomi team requirements for full verification control
- No direct production/logistics system connection requiring manual transfers
- Time tracking integration functional but limited in scope
- Manual processes exist by design (Ekonomi) and by gap (production systems)

**Integration Priority:** Medium - Production integration would add value; banking/accounting automation not desired by stakeholders

**Comparison:** Idus faces a critical broken integration requiring immediate attention with 1.55M SEK annual impact. Agda/Visma's manual processes are partly intentional (banking/accounting per Ekonomi team requirements) and partly gaps (production systems). Idus needs urgent restoration and repair; Agda/Visma needs strategic evaluation of which manual processes should be automated versus maintained for control purposes.

Both indicate training and change management gaps rather than fundamental usability problems.

## Key Patterns Across Systems

**Common Strengths**
- Both systems rated highly critical (9/10 and 10/10) by users
- Strong core functionality in primary use cases
- Proven stability and reliability in daily operations
- Super user advocacy for continued investment over replacement

**Common Weaknesses**
- Integration gaps requiring manual workarounds (though vastly different scales)
- Unutilized or underutilized features representing waste
- Super user dependencies creating single-point-of-failure risks
- Training and change management gaps affecting adoption

**Critical Insight**
Systems rated highly by users may harbor efficiency drains at dramatically different scales. Idus's 4.7M SEK annual waste versus Agda/Visma's 75K SEK demonstrates that technical failures create 63x greater financial impact than organizational adoption issues. This suggests that while both system investment and organizational capability development matter, unresolved technical problems pose exponentially higher financial risk and require urgent prioritization.

## Management Implications

**Strategic Pattern Recognition**
Both completed assessments reveal systems with substantial efficiency losses, though at dramatically different scales. Idus's 4.7M SEK annual waste is approximately 63 times higher than Agda/Visma's 75K SEK, representing fundamentally different problem urgency levels. Idus requires immediate technical intervention while Agda/Visma needs organizational improvement focused on employee self-service adoption.

**Investment Philosophy**
Despite the 4.7M SEK annual waste, Tom (Idus super user) strongly recommends investing in the existing system rather than replacement, citing strong core CMMS capabilities. This bottom-up recommendation aligns with cost-benefit analysis showing targeted improvements (SAP integration restoration, reporting module, mobile platform) offer better ROI than platform replacement. Helena's recommendation for Agda/Visma follows similar logic - invest in training and adoption rather than technology replacement.

**Organizational Capability Gaps**
The dramatic cost difference (4.7M vs 75K SEK) reveals that technical system failures create exponentially higher waste than organizational adoption issues. Idus's broken SAP integration alone (1.55M SEK/year) exceeds Agda/Visma's total waste by 21x. This suggests that while both organizational and technical capabilities matter, unresolved technical failures pose far greater financial risk.

**Resource Allocation Priorities**
1. **Urgent (Idus):** Restore SAP integration (1.55M SEK/year savings)
2. **High (Idus):** Deploy mobile platform (981K SEK/year savings)  
3. **High (Idus):** Implement reporting module (176K SEK/year savings)
4. **Medium (Agda):** Self-service adoption campaign (42-83K SEK/year savings)
5. **Medium (Agda):** Evaluate production system integration feasibility

**Note:** Banking and accounting integrations for Agda/Visma are intentionally manual per Ekonomi team requirements and not included in improvement priorities.

**Financial Risk Assessment**
Idus represents 98.4% of the combined system waste (4.7M of 4.775M SEK total), creating concentrated financial risk requiring immediate management attention. Agda/Visma's waste, while important, represents manageable operational inefficiency rather than critical financial drain.
